FP4 claim-to-evidence audit and manuscript wording
2026-10-21; evidence frozen at commit 10580d6638ad82f74667d8d6616baeecfe85c12b

Decision

The strongest upgrade is a sharp, contract-specific precision characterization
with small complete computer-assisted proofs. The original p53 correction
elimination claim was already established; its truth is not newly discovered
by replacing a large certificate with a smaller one. The p45 normal and p49
full-domain boundaries add information beyond the original single-p53 claim.
The smaller certificates improve proof dependencies and inspection, but do not
by themselves establish a new general rounding theorem, publication novelty,
runtime gain or a hardware precision requirement.

No arithmetic, certificate checker, direct-j enumerator, prefix, representative
set or historical sweep was rerun in this audit. Existing result counts below
are evidence descriptions, not new observations. evidence_index.json records
the bytes/SHA256 of the referenced files. Source theorem premises and current
availability are in theorem_source_matrix.json. The symbolic-proof decision
is in symbolic_proof_value.txt.

Common contract B

B means finite nonnegative BF16 magnitudes 0<=x<=a, s=RN32(a/6) with ties
to even and gradual underflow, zero fallback2^-42, E2M1 grid
(0,1/2,1,3/2,2,3,4,6), and the unchanged b32 probability lattice. C(y) is
the canonical interval/base plus floor(2^32 times interval probability),
after clipping the magnitude ratio at6. Virtual RNE-p uses binary64 exponent
range and integer35<=p<=64. N and S restrict B to normal and positive
subnormal derived scales. Zero is handled separately. No independent F32
scale, FTZ, changed random-word policy, NaN/infinity domain, native p45/p49
instruction, or arbitrary real-valued SR contract is admitted.

Evidence IDs resolve to repository-relative paths and hashes in the index.
Reference to an earlier result is not a claim that it ran in this audit.

C01. The final compatibility correction is redundant at p53

Manuscript location: p1 Abstract/Contributions; p2 arithmetic and domain
argument; p5 conclusion. Domain: B at b32,p53, for the specified scalar
arithmetic and unchanged surrounding semantics.
Proof/evidence: E_HISTORY_DOMAIN and E_HISTORY_RESULT preserve the complete
532701120-pair CPU result; E_HISTORY_LEMMA gives the normal analytic mechanism.
E_NORMAL_PROOF/E_NORMAL_CERT/E_NORMAL_RESULTS and E_S49_PROOF/E_R49_INDEPENDENT
now supply smaller sufficient proof dependencies via p45/p49 and monotonicity.
E_S53_PROOF/E_S53_REVIEW separately provide the conservative subnormal p53
elementary argument and informal review. These are alternatives, not new
independent workloads to add to the old pair count.
Standard part: exact interpolation, midpoint separation, precision monotonicity
and composition from equal canonical base/threshold.
Additional obligation: coupled divisor/input/lattice separation, discharged
by the stated domain proofs/certificates. Native execution equivalence needs
the separately preserved source/binary/output evidence; scalar mathematics
is not a CUDA concurrency or compiler proof.
Allowed: "Under the specified BF16/derived-scale contract, the final RN64
compatibility correction is unnecessary; the scalar result has analytic and
complete finite proof components."
Exclude: "The correction is unnecessary for arbitrary F32 inputs/scales";
"a new universal stochastic-rounding algorithm"; "formally verified CUDA".
OPEN: maximal valid domain, necessity of every assumption, formal development.

There are1600 A,t branches,2814 retained J/eta branches,236 residue solutions:
234 violate X128..255 and two violate eta=1[X*65536<D]. The proof no longer
depends on the old630848 normal representative evaluations.
Standard part: quotient/remainder algebra, gcd and complete residue classes.
Additional part: q/A divisor classes from RN32(a/6), the b32 lattice and exact
binade relation L+J=61+eta. E_NORMAL_ALGEBRA_REVIEW reviews the stronger bound

certificate."

or faster"; "all FP4 algorithms need at least45 arithmetic bits".

C03. Sharp normal lower bound from a p44 failure

Domain: the same normal virtual quotient-rounding family and p35..64, not
all possible exact quantizer implementations. Evidence: E_FIXED_ARITHMETIC,
E_NORMAL_PROOF section6, E_OLD_PROPOSITIONS. Legal a code492,x code199 has
D10310997,j2845; j*2^11<=D<j*2^12. The p44 canonical thresholds differ by1,
and the same carry argument refutes p35..44. The witness was known before
the latest proof, not prospectively discovered by the new reduction.
Standard part: one legal counterexample plus monotonic crossing and C02.
Additional obligation: verify legality and exact arithmetic; covered by the
preserved identity and direct negative control. No prefix scan was rerun.
Allowed: "Within p35..64,45 is the uniform minimum for this normal-scale
quotient-rounding predicate."
Exclude: "A universal lower bound for FP4 hardware/algorithms"; "the first
counterexample in every ordering". Raw-code lexicographic minimality is a
separate inherited ordering result, not the reason the precision minimum is45.
OPEN: no assertion for p<35 or alternative arithmetic algorithms.

C04. Subnormal p49 and the independently implemented reduced certificate

Domain: S under B. Proof/evidence: E_S49_PROOF, E_R49_OLD_LEDGER,
E_R49_INDEPENDENT, E_R49_BRANCHES, E_R49_FREEZE and E_R49_COMPARISON.
The independently written direct-j implementation ran once:4656 A,t branches,
124640 direct congruence checks, one coarse survivor and six reconstructions.
The survivor requires X=531 at u15 and noninteger X for u>15; no legal input
remains. Every branch parameter, survivor and reconstruction agrees with the
older inverse-based certificate. The99 epsilon-zero diagnostic congruence hits
are excluded by exact-scale divisibility, not suppressed counterexamples.
Standard part: integer congruence, divisibility and full-equation reconstruction.
Additional part: unnormalized subnormal units, legal coefficient/v classes,
j caps42/85/127, t caps16/4/1 and safe modulus exponents15/15/13.
Allowed: "A separately implemented complete reduced-condition enumeration
agrees with the subnormal p49 certificate."
Exclude: "blind independent replication"; "proof-assistant verification";
"4656 physical input pairs tested"; "the congruence has no solutions".
OPEN: a certificate-free general exclusion and common-specification risk.
The prior conclusion was known to the auditor and disclosed before the run.

C05. Full-domain p49 boundary and the original p53 corollary

Domain: B and virtual p35..64. Proof form: computer-assisted composition of
C02 for normal scales, C04 for subnormal scales, exact zero/saturation cases,
and the inherited legal subnormal p48 witness a242,x221. Evidence:
E_NORMAL_PROOF sections6..8, E_S49_PROOF sections6..7, E_OLD_PROPOSITIONS and
E_STATUS. Necessity uses the witness; sufficiency uses the complete reductions.
The old large normal/subnormal safety certificates are no longer required by
this proof route, although they remain preserved corroboration.
Standard part: subset composition and precision monotonicity. The additional
contract-specific work is exactly C02/C04, not an extra generic theorem.
Allowed: "For the fixed b32 contract and p35..64, the full-domain uniform
threshold-preservation boundary is49; the proof is computer-assisted."
Exclude: "p45 is safe for the entire BF16 domain"; "49 is required by every
correct FP4 implementation"; "all-input pure symbolic proof".
OPEN: certificate-free proofs, other scale policies, formats or p ranges.

C07. Every-word scalar equivalence and observed native outputs

Manuscript location: p2 composition, p3..4 correctness/evaluation. Domain:
equal scale/base/T with unchanged sign, packet addresses, prefix/lazy policy,
packing and consumer semantics. Evidence: E_HISTORY_DOMAIN composition,
E_MANUSCRIPT p3..4 and E_GPU_PROTOCOL. Equal thresholds give equal U<T for
every fixed logical U32 word; this is a standard deterministic corollary.
The preserved105 native cases before/after timing support observed equality
for their captured arrays; they do not enumerate every CUDA execution.
Allowed: "Canonical equality preserves every logical-word scalar decision
under unchanged surrounding semantics; archived native tests corroborate the
specified implementation."
Exclude: "The scalar theorem proves GPU race freedom, all compiler outputs,
or every end-to-end model execution". No native output was collected here.
OPEN: formal composition with implementation/concurrency semantics.

C08. Exact threshold compatibility versus unbiased real SR

Domain: unsaturated scalar FP4 interval and a uniform U32 word. Evidence:
E_HISTORY_DOMAIN, section 'Composition and a limited corollary'. An exact
floor at32 probability bits has the familiar downward limited-random-bit
expectation bound; equal legacy thresholds are not exact real-probability SR.
Standard part: elementary finite-lattice probability arithmetic. This is not
new bias theory. Saturation, BF16 reconstruction, residual addition, stream
correlation and model quality require separate treatment.
Allowed: "Bit-exact compatibility concerns canonical finite-word thresholds."
Exclude: "Exactly unbiased real stochastic rounding"; "quality or statistical
independence follows from seedwise equality".
OPEN: application/statistical properties outside this finite-word predicate.

C09. Modest implementation gain and the stronger baseline

Manuscript location: p1 Abstract; p4 Tables3/4 and Fig1; p5 limitations.
Evidence: E_MANUSCRIPT, E_GPU_TABLE, E_GPU_PROTOCOL. These are historical
sessions, not new measurements. The manuscript reports producer reductions
over corrected B of3.15% on SXM and3.65% on NVL; D1 remains slower than
strong FP64 A-double-prime on ordinary/model-derived and Gaussian inputs.
The NVL core producer comparison reports D1 taking16.81% longer than that
strong baseline. Synthetic repeated edges are a distinct fixed condition.
Standard part: numerical correctness permits deletion of work but gives no
latency bound. Additional runtime evidence is empirical and session-specific.
Allowed: "The archived implementation removes redundant work and shows a
modest gain over corrected B in the specified sessions; stronger-baseline
rankings remain input-dependent."
Exclude: "The precision proof produces a new speedup"; "universally faster
quantizer"; "public-library or end-to-end LLM superiority"; "hardware need".
OPEN: runtime value of any new implementation, mechanism attribution and
workload-family generality. No comparison is recomputed or pooled here.

precision characterization; derivability from all closest theory and

Exclude: "Roux/Saiki/Rump cannot derive this result"; "worldwide first";
"a new general double-rounding framework"; access failure as originality.
OPEN: exact Rump Lemma4.6 premises/conclusion/version and applicability;
full-framework derivability and broader literature priority.

Source-status corrections required before a manuscript revision

E_HISTORY_PRIOR_ART records Roux's PDF body as not inspected at that older
review. A later study actually obtained the publisher PDF and text, whose
hashes and Theorem29 location are now available. Those are different reading
times. The older report is preserved; its old access status is not promoted
to a current gap, nor silently rewritten as a historical successful reading.

The preserved p5 manuscript and E_HISTORY_PRIOR_ART say that a Rump author
draft/Lemma4.6 was inspected historically. Current preserved exact-source
coverage does not reproduce that inspection: there is no checked lemma body,
source-file hash, exact premises/conclusion or verified draft/final match in
the current study. This is an unresolved provenance gap, not proof that the
old reviewer was wrong or that the lemma is inapplicable.

Do not carry the manuscript's combined claim about Roux AND Rump's inspected
premises forward as if both were currently verified. Separate the verified
Roux29 mapping from the pending Rump comparison. Suggested wording:
"Roux's named nearest-rounding theorem does not directly instantiate our
fixed-lattice floor target. A premise-level comparison with Rump's exact
Lemma4.6 remains pending recovery of the relevant primary text."

The manuscript also cites historically inspected Lean source revision8bd80452.
This audit uses the actually preserved Saiki v1 HTML/Figure8; it does not have
newly checked named Lean lemma bodies or a current file identity for that
code. Qualify the Lean inspection as historical, or restore the exact pinned
files before retaining precise code-level premise assertions. Do not convert
HTML inspection into a claim that Lean was executed.

The exact historical04_FINAL_NOVELTY_AND_LIMITATIONS.md remains unavailable.
E_HISTORY_PRIOR_ART is a different, available report and is not its substitute.
Fasi/Mikaitis, CPFloat, KBench and other historical sources remain relevant
antecedents, but their theorem bodies were not freshly obtained in this audit;
the historical review is a secondary pointer, not a newly verified exclusion.

Recommended paper change and next step

Retain the original implementation evidence and unfavorable strong-baseline
comparisons. Add a clearly labelled computer-assisted theorem describing the
normal45 and full49 boundaries in the specified virtual-precision family,
with the p44/p48 witnesses and exact certificate dependencies. Update proof
status in the limitations, and qualify unreproduced Rump/Lean source assertions.
Do not advertise a new native lower-precision kernel or merge b37 into the
new small-certificate claim.

The next bounded step is a proposed theory/limitations section using these
allowed sentences and proof dependencies. Do that before prioritizing more
symbolic search. A symbolic proof under identical fixed assumptions changes
proof form, not the truth or generality already supplied by the complete
certificates; its further value must meet the gates in symbolic_proof_value.txt.
