Incremental value of a certificate-free symbolic proof
2026-10-21

Decision: DEFER_AS_PRIMARY_RESEARCH_OBJECTIVE.
Use the existing small complete certificates for the fixed-contract claims.
The symbolic exclusion remains mathematically interesting, but no demonstrated
incremental publication contribution justifies further same-domain computation
as the default next step. This is a priority decision, not a claim that the
open proof is impossible or worthless, and not a permanent project stop.

Current baseline

Normal45 has a complete2814-row branch certificate and236 candidate exclusions
after the1600 A,t reduction. The inverse-free verifier checks complete residue
classes and supplied identities, so the generator's modular-inverse routine
is not a trusted numerical oracle. Subnormal49 has a4656-branch certificate
and one impossible reconstruction, with a separately implemented direct-j
run agreeing across all branches. The full49 consequence is already true
with those finite proof components. No further input search is needed to
decide that fixed finite predicate.

The old630848 normal representative evaluations,100575 subnormal pairs and
532701120-pair historical sweep have different units and roles. Removing a
large-sweep proof dependency is a structural improvement, not a measured
checker speedup equal to a ratio of those counts. The smaller proof route
does not erase the historical evidence. No checker timing was measured here.

1. Generality

A symbolic proof with exactly the same BF16, RN32(a/6), b32 and p35..64
assumptions proves the same statement. It does not automatically cover
arbitrary F32 scales, FTZ, other input significand widths, probability bits,
scale divisors, exponent contracts or RNG policies.
Potential additional value: a parameterized separation theorem predicting a
precision boundary for a predeclared family of coefficient widths/lattices or
scale rules. Current status: NOT DEMONSTRATED. A new family must be stated
and compared with existing theory before experiments; changing proof notation
or calling a finite table a theorem is not such an extension.

2. Assumption reduction

Potential additional value: prove a strict superset or remove a specific
assumption with a complete implication argument. Merely not using x<=a at
the final residue-filter stage does not prove a larger complete quantizer
contract; earlier bin, exponent, clipping and scale assumptions still matter.
Current status: no maximal-domain result and no proof that every current
assumption is necessary. A broader theorem remains OPEN, not a consequence
of these certificates' zero mismatches.

3. Proof/checking cost and trust

Potential additional value: fewer independently checkable arithmetic cases,
a shorter proof artifact, or a formal proof with a clearly specified trusted
base. Measure bytes, proof cases and checker work on the same statement and
environment before claiming a cost reduction. No such new reduction is
measured here. A proof assistant could also verify the existing finite
certificate, so "symbolic" alone does not uniquely provide formal assurance.
Current status: structural reduction from historical sweeps is established;
additional savings from a future symbolic closure are NOT ESTABLISHED.

4. Explanation and readability

Existing explanations already identify 3D=A*2^q+epsilon, the J-dependent t
bound, and eta as the guard excluding the two normalized-X survivors. A
short nonenumerative exclusion could make the last236-case closure easier
to inspect and teach. That would be a real expository improvement, plausibly
useful as a proof appendix, but it is not automatically a new research result.
Current status: PLAUSIBLE_MODEST_BENEFIT; no concise closure is available.

5. Original correction-removal claim and performance

For p53 the correction-removal property was already supported by an analytic
normal argument and complete CPU evidence. New boundary theorems and compact
subnormal proofs improve characterization and transparency; they do not turn
the old p53 result into a newly discovered fact. Nor does a symbolic proof
change the captured CUDA binary, remove integer division, improve the D1 vs
strong A-double-prime ranking, or establish model quality. Further runtime
claims would need separately authorized implementation/evaluation work.

Conditions for resuming symbolic work as the primary objective

Before more search, select at least one concrete deliverable:
G1: a genuinely broader, explicitly parameterized domain/theorem;
G2: a specific removed assumption and a proven strict-superset inclusion;
G3: a quantified reduction of proof/checking work or a formal assurance target;
G4: a short explanatory closure whose editorial value justifies the effort,
    while explicitly retaining a non-novel-exposition label if appropriate.
Also state the strongest existing theorem that might subsume that deliverable.
The unrecovered exact Rump lemma prevents closing this comparison; lack of
access cannot be converted into a favorable answer.

Current recommendation and one next step

Do not rerun the completed certificates or chase more residues in this phase.
Prepare a proposed theory/limitations manuscript revision centered on the
computer-assisted45/49 characterization, with an explicit evidence dependency
diagram/table and corrected source-status qualifications. That is immediately
useful, reviewable work even if Rump remains unavailable. Reassess symbolic
work only against a chosen gate, rather than treating it as required to
establish a fixed-domain truth already settled by complete certificates.
